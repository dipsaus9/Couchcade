## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
Target Range's aim now streams single { yaw, pitch } samples (3 decimals) through the controller's one InputChannel at 30 Hz (streams: { aim: { hz: 30 } }), replacing the CC-3.6 packed-array stream and its CC-3.21 shim; shoot and lower fire on the same channel, and shoot carries channel.last("aim") as the aim the TV was shown. Aim speed moved to the owner's 6 world px per degree in both directions (aimPxPerDegree in shared/constants.ts, yawRangeDeg 33.3 / pitchRangeDeg 15 passed to createAimDetector) and the touch pad to 1.5 world px per CSS px both ways (padPxPerCssPx, derived yawDragPx/pitchDragPx passed to createAimDrag). The TV scene now plays each crosshair back with the generic createPlayback at the link's per-player playbackDelayMs (new host/aim-playback.ts's createCrosshairPlayback, called from scene.ts's Phaser update(time, delta)) and snaps it to a shot's aim over 60 ms, with present() reduced to threading the already-computed crosshairs through so it stays a pure function of state. AC6's tuning check (test/controller/aim-tuning.test.ts) replayed synthetic hold/sweep/release traces (no real trace recorded yet -- owner's pnpm trace:record is a separate follow-up) and recorded p90 hold wobble 2.15 px, reach 13.36 degrees and release twitch 11.60 px in the task notes, both under the owner's 3 px / 20 degree thresholds, so aimPxPerDegree stays at 6 with no escalation needed. Updated every affected test (rules, contract, determinism, bots, helpers, controller aim/Controller, present, plus two new test files) and the e2e bot-match spec's touch-pad pixel constants; the e2e spec passed on chromium and webkit. docs/games/target-range.md's aim numbers are out of this story's References (games/target-range/src/, games/target-range/test/, e2e/games/target-range.spec.ts) and are left as a follow-up.
