## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Root cause found at pickup (headless Chromium screenshots): StageScene sets text resolution to max(1, overlayZoom). The canvas is window × devicePixelRatio, so a 1080p TV with a non-fullscreen browser (about 1920×970) gets world zoom ×3 and overlay zoom 0.75; glyphs are rasterised at 1080p size and point-sampled down by nearest-neighbour (pixelArt), which drops and doubles strokes. With a fractional zoom above 1 (HiDPI laptops, 4K TVs with scaling) Phaser skips vertex rounding (onlyTranslate is false under a zoomed camera), so text quads land between pixels. Only a fullscreen 1920×1080 or 3840×2160 canvas was sharp.
Verify: pnpm --filter @couchcade/stage test; games/quick-draw and games/target-range browser tests; screenshots via a scratch harness into scratchpad/shots/cc-10.10.

Review gate round 1 (dipsaus-ai:story-reviewer, sonnet): verdict pass. AC1-6 met, no scope violations, no findings. The reviewer reverted src/scene/index.ts to main and saw both new cases fail (42% of glyph pixels off at x0.75; texture 1 px narrower than its quad at x1.5). The task file and pnpm-lock.yaml (unchanged) were left out of the reviewer diff on purpose.

## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
Overlay text on the TV is now drawn 1:1 on canvas pixels at every canvas size, which fixes the soft, uneven scoreboard text the owner saw. Root cause: StageScene rasterised overlay text at max(1, overlay zoom) and the stage uses nearest-neighbour textures, so any canvas with a world zoom under x4 (a 1080p TV in a browser window is 1920x970: world x3, overlay x0.75; 720p is x0.5) point-sampled 1080p glyphs down and dropped or doubled strokes; at fractional zooms above x1 Phaser skipped vertex rounding and the text canvas was a fraction of a pixel smaller than its quad. StageScene now calls sharpenText on every overlay text before each frame: resolution = overlay zoom exactly, upright text (itself and its containers not rotated or scaled) uses vertexRoundMode full, and the right and bottom padding are topped up by under one canvas pixel so the texture is a whole number of pixels (sized with Phaser's own GetTextSize sums, redone only when text, size or zoom change). Rotated or scaling text such as callouts keeps Phaser's default rounding. Being in @couchcade/stage, Target Range gets the same fix. Tests: a browser test renders scoreboard names at x0.75 and x1.5 and compares every glyph pixel with the text's own texture over Chalk (fails on the old code); stage, Quick Draw and Target Range suites pass. platform.md TV rendering gains the rule. Before/after screenshots at 1920x1080, 1920x970 and 1280x720 are listed in the PR.
