## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
Quick Draw now accepts 1 to 8 players. With one seated player it runs as solo practice against the player's own best time. There is no opponent and no bot. The round flow, timings, fakes from round 2 and fouls are unchanged. Every valid tap scores 1 point. The match ends after the round that reaches 3 points, after round 9, or when the player's seat expires. A valid time is a new best when it is the first valid time or strictly faster than the best before it. State gains an optional practice { totalMs, validTaps, newBest } only for one-player matches. The snapshot saves practice [totalMs, validTaps], and qd-result gains practice { newBest, bestMs, averageMs, final }. Matches with 2 or more players keep their state, views, snapshots and the recorded replay byte for byte. TV panel: 'New best: 0.243', '0.301, best 0.243' or 'No time this round'; the last round shows 'Best 0.243 · average 0.271'. Phone: 'New best!' or 'Your best is 0.243 s', with the best and average as the hint on the last round. The spec gains a Solo practice section with pointers from At a glance, rule 1 and the README row. Tests: test/solo.test.ts (9 unit tests), a browser scene test for a one-player match, and e2e/games/quick-draw-solo.spec.ts (one phone plays a full match to the results screen).
