## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
Built games/strike-night/src/host/: the 480x270 world in two camera shots (approach behind the foul line, cut to the pin deck when the ball's world y passes 16, staying there through result), with the ball, pins, bowler and bench via CC-6.4's World Pips. StrikeNightScene extends StageScene and uses the stage overlays (scoreboard, round counter, room code panel, STRIKE!/SPARE!/TURKEY! callouts) plus game-local ones (bottom panel + clock, pin map, scorecard). layout.ts's camera projections are checked against the spec's own worked numbers (foul line at screen y=240, head pin at y=96, etc). A headless boot test drives a full 1-player match to its outcome via auto-rolls only, asserting no errors. Simple palette-colour primitives stand in for CC-12.5's CC0 sprites -- this story owns positioning and mechanics, not final art. Reviewer (sonnet) round 1 blocked on the scorecard's mark text not matching the spec's worked examples and missing the player shape mark; both fixed and round 2 passed clean (plus a voluntary follow-up: the scorecard's shape now keys off the platform's own PlayerInfo.slot, matching the World Pips and scoreboard chips, per round 2's advisory note). Screenshots at 1920x1080 captured via a throwaway debug harness (not committed) for the delivery report.
