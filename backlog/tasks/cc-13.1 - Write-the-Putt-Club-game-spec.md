## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Owner gate: open the docs PR, send the owner a short summary plus the PR link in chat, and wait for explicit approval. Merge only after approval; record it with --append-notes.
Inspiration: Wii Sports "Golf" putting and mini-golf games like Mario Golf. Controls idea: hold the grip, swing like a putter; power from swing speed; swipe fallback. Research code examples before writing the spec.

Independent review (dipsaus-ai:story-reviewer, sonnet, round 1): verdict pass, no scope violations, one advisory. Criterion 1 met: the reviewer cross-checked every API and constant claim against packages/motion/src/gestures/{swing,aim}.ts, packages/motion/src/fallbacks/{swing,aim}.ts, packages/physics/src/{helpers,types}.ts, packages/game-sdk/src/contract/index.ts and packages/theme/src/scenes/, confirmed the 'green' palette id does not collide, resolved every internal anchor and cross-doc link, checked the quoted motion.md excerpts verbatim, and reproduced the weight/roll table, the tolerance table, all six worked-numbers rows, the 6.9 s stroke, the 64.5 s overhead and the four-row match-length table in node. The one advisory (the worst-case budget row quoted a duration and an hourly rate without the workings, so the two did not obviously agree) is fixed in 9bbf6d0: 216 strokes at 19.2 s is 70.2 minutes, and 13,392 phone messages plus 452 controller:state plus 20 room messages is 13,864, or about 11,850 an hour. Criterion 2 is unmet by design: owner-gate, the approval must come from the owner.

Two API details were also corrected before the review landed (ec9ae91): wallLoop and wallPath take a point list, not a wall list; and createSwingSwipe has no minPeak option, its floor being minSwipePx.

Open questions for the owner, called out in the spec:
- Finding 10, the one worth a decision: other players' balls are ghosts, not obstacles. Only the putter's ball is a physics body, so nobody loses a stroke to a ball someone else left in the way and the physics halves. The alternative -- four live balls knocking each other about -- is funnier and would make turn order matter much more. Recommendation: ghosts for v1; switching is a change to CC-13.2's world build and nothing else in the spec moves.
- Finding 12: 4-player matches land at about 13.1 minutes typical, the second-longest game in the catalogue behind Strike Night's 14. The 6-stroke cap is what bounds it and the turn timer is the only other knob, because 9 holes is the epic's shape and CC-13.8's contract. CC-13.7's playtest decides whether turnTimerMs comes down from 15 s.
- Finding 4: Putt Club is the first game to recentre the touch drag pad at the game's own recentre moment, which motion.md's approved CC-5.12 recommendation point 7 asks for and Target Range does not yet do. A small follow-up story would bring Target Range in line.

Verified on 2026-09-21: pnpm check, pnpm check:style, pnpm check:deps, pnpm test and pnpm build all green (docs-only). Every internal anchor and cross-doc link resolves.
