## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Verify: pnpm e2e

Round 2 fixes: improved e2e test robustness - reduced timeout from 360s to 120s, added stall detection, better swipe gesture, stronger assertions on winner text

Round 5: Fixed button element targeting - target actual CcBigAction button nested in div.grip, not div.grip bounding box. Scope note: meta.ts (which un-hides the game) is outside original References but is necessary for AC1.

Round 6 (root-cause fix): the e2e test was reading a field named `bowler` off the TV state, but the real StrikeNightState has no such field (it's `bowlerId`, a player id) -- so the ternary always fell through to Ben/motion regardless of whose turn it actually was. Motion-mode bowls also never held the CcBigAction button: bowl.ts's swing detector only listens between pointer grip-down/grip-up marks, so playing a motion trace alone (no grip) was silently ignored. The background 'hold still' motion trace for Ben also ran concurrently with his real swing trace on the same fake adapter, which could interleave samples into one swing detector. Finally, the loop's stall detector compared turn numbers on every iteration (including while a roll was still animating through rolling/result, which legitimately keeps the same turn number), and its 'wait for lineup' branch didn't accept the match ending in 'over' instead of returning to 'lineup', and its iteration budget (30) assumed roll counts for one player, not two players sharing every frame (worst case 40 rolls). Fixed: resolve bowler by id->name lookup; hold the button through grip-down/grip-up around the motion swing trace; pause the background still-trace while a real swing plays; only compare turn numbers between two lineup observations; accept 'over' as a valid exit from the lineup wait; raise the iteration budget to 90 with a trailing poll for the final roll's tail. Verified: chromium and webkit each pass in isolation on two separate clean runs (~5 minutes each, real 10-frame match, no flakiness observed).
