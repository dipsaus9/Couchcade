## Implementation Plan

<!-- SECTION:PLAN:BEGIN -->
Wire CC-11.5's sprites into the CC-11.4 scene the way Quick Draw does (?inline data URIs, loadSprites in preload): art.ts holds the sprite roles and the few drawn colours from core + range; world.ts builds the backdrop from grass/pine/hedge/fence/hay-bale tiles, the target from the stand + face sprites, the wind flag from the flag strips (on top of the boss), bows and arrows from their sprites with the fletching swapped to the player's colour; crosshairs and World Pips stay drawn (no sprite). layout.ts gets the stand geometry. Keep CC-4.11 camera rules and CC-11.4 label layout. Iterate on 1920x1080 screenshots.
<!-- SECTION:PLAN:END -->

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Gap found by the orchestrator 2026-09-17: CC-11.4 built the scene with core-colour placeholders through host/art.ts roles so CC-11.5's art could drop in, and CC-11.5 added the sprites and palette, but no story wires them together. Follow Quick Draw's CC-10.8 pattern (sprites inlined/loaded in the scene) and keep the CC-4.11 rendering rules. Keep the game hidden; CC-11.6 unhides it.

Scope amendment 2026-09-17 (worker): the CC-11.5 target face sprites could not be used as drawn. Their bands ran gold, red, blue, black, white from the edge in (the spec and the scoring say white, black, blue, red, gold, 10 in the gold centre), and the far face was 44 px across for a radius-24 target, so arrows scoring 1 would land off the painted face. Redrew target-near/middle/far.png from the rules' ring formula (ring k reaches R x (11-k)/10, radius 36/30/24, odd size centred on a pixel, 1px Ink outline) on 80/72/56 px canvases; still on the core + range palette and drawn from scratch (CREDITS.md unchanged). Added the three files to References; no in-flight story touches games/target-range/assets.

Review gate (dipsaus-ai:story-reviewer, round 1): pass. AC1-3 met, no scope violations (References as amended). Advisories: (1) faceKey fell back to the near face for an unknown radius -> fixed, it now throws; (2) art roles index the range palette by position -> left as is, check:style's sprite palette check and the browser pixel tests catch a drift; (3) the wind flag now stands on top of the straw boss (as on real ranges, so it reads above the grass), while the spec's TV scene row says next to the target, and the round-1 face covers the whole boss -> spec wording follow-up for docs/games/target-range.md, outside this story's References.
Screenshots: 1920x1080, 4 and 8 players (intro, aiming, arrows flying, arrows landed, round result, round 4) taken with a local-only Vitest browser test, inspected and iterated (stub outline, fence layout); not committed. TV scene chunk (apps/host build): 30.99 kB / 14.42 kB gzip, was 21.82 kB / 8.53 kB with placeholders; pnpm budgets green. Face PNGs written as indexed PNG (319-411 B).
