1. Everyone draws at once, no bracket. Follow-up: update the README Players column.
2. The Pips draw toy cork popguns with a BANG! flag.
3. Fake-out calls are in the first version (against the recommendation).
Spec updated: the open questions are replaced by an Owner decisions section and a new Fake-outs section. Fakes: look-alike words (DRIP!, DRUM!, DROP!, DRY!, DREAM!, DRIFT!), a crow caw, a popgun glint. None in round 1; from round 2, 0/1/2 fakes at 30/50/20%. Each fake lands at least 1,000 ms after the standoff starts, before DRAW! and from other fakes. A tap on a fake is a normal foul (out of the round, points never taken away) with a 'fooled' message. Fakes are TV-only and add no messages. Early foul views are dropped, so the host sends exactly 2 controller:state per round (about 11 requests per round with 8 players).
