## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
A western reaction duel and the first playable game: wait for DRAW, then tap first. Early is a foul, and so is tapping on a fake-out call.

Inspired by: 1-2-Switch "Quick Draw" and Kirby Super Star "Samurai Kirby".
Controls: tap the big action when the TV shouts DRAW; timing judged on the phone with clock sync.
Players: 2 to 8, everyone draws at once (owner decision 2026-09-16, no bracket). Pips draw toy popguns; fake-out calls are in the first version (see docs/games/quick-draw.md). Scene palette: desert.
