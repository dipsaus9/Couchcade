## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Owner gate: open the docs PR, send the owner a short summary plus the PR link in chat, and wait for explicit approval. Merge only after approval; record it with --append-notes.
Inspiration: Wii Sports "Bowling". Controls idea: hold the grip button, swing back and forward, release; twist for spin; swipe fallback. Research code examples before writing the spec.

Research (2026-09-17): Wii Sports bowling controls from Wikibooks (D-pad move, A+D-pad angle, hold B, swing, release B, tilt on release for spin); Switch Sports (hold ZR, twist wrist to hook, move/rotate before bowling, up to 4 local, Special lanes); Wii Sports Resort (MotionPlus spin, 100-pin); Kinect Sports (reach, swing, exaggerate for spin); World Bowling current frame scoring from Wikipedia Ten-pin bowling; lane/pin/ball dimensions and hook in the last ~20 ft; web-bowling (tvalletta) as phone-as-ball precedent on Box2D. CC0 licences checked in downloads (Kenney Impact/Interface/Music Jingles/Roguelike Indoors License.txt, qubodup bowling roll 7z contents) and on source pages (Funky Menu Loop, Happy Adventure). Rejected CC-BY/CC-BY-SA bowling assets.
Prototype (scratchpad only, not committed): ball + 10 pins on @couchcade/physics. Plain disc pins gave ~5% strikes over a sweep of lines; a 'falling pin' radius 0.06->0.08 m once speed > 0.5 m/s gave 19% strikes, 7.3 pins avg, pocket hits 53% strikes, head-on 6-7 pins; release to pins 2.15-4.78 s; ~5.5 ms CPU per roll. Constants recorded in the spec as CC-12.2 starting values.
Spec choices: turn timer 20 s + 500 ms wait then auto-roll (speed 0.3 straight), away rule after 2 auto-rolls (5 s turns); swipe fallback over tap (tap drops speed and hook); 3 owner questions: scoring, match length/player count, aim method.

Review gate round 1 (dipsaus-ai:story-reviewer): verdict pass. AC1 met; AC2 (owner approval) open by design; no scope violations. Five advisory findings fixed in a follow-up commit: pin shot depth scale now gives 12 px rows (sy = 170 - (y - 18.29) x 45), ball frames up to 32x32 with a 16x16 cap in the approach shot, bullet renamed to 'Speed doesn't change the hook', snapshot drops positions (session-flow snapshot rule 4), away-player cost restated as 5.5 s vs 20.5 s of lining up. Task file and pnpm-lock.yaml excluded from the reviewer diff on purpose.

Approved by owner: 2026-09-17
Owner decisions: (1) current frame scoring, as recommended; (2) 1 to 4 players, always 10 frames, as the README says (not the recommendation): frame scaling, 5-8 players and the two-lane layout dropped, match length about 14 min for 4 players, budget recomputed (about 250 requests for 2 players, 500 for 4), turn cap 80; the README/epic follow-up is no longer needed; (3) aim with stand position plus swing direction nudge, as recommended. Spec status set to approved with an Owner decisions section at the end.
