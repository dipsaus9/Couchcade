## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
games/quick-draw/src/host/ now holds the Quick Draw TV scene. It is a Phaser scene loaded through the game's hostScene loader, which replaces the rejecting stub. Every frame it reads HostSceneData.getState() and draws the 480x270 desert street with placeholder shapes in @couchcade/theme colours: Pips in seat colour and shape, a scoreboard, the round chip, the bottom panel, DRAW! and fake-word callouts, the crow and the popgun glint, FOUL! and reaction-time tags, BANG! flags, the tumbleweed and the dust puff. It honours reduced motion. Presentation (present.ts) and sound cues (cues.ts, emitted as 'quick-draw:cue' for CC-10.5) are pure and unit-tested. A Vitest browser-mode boot test in headless Chromium steps Phaser one tick per frame. It renders one full round with the canvas at 480x270 and zoom x2, checks pixels and sees no console errors. It also plays a whole 8-player match with all three fake kinds, and a reduced-motion run. The scene extends Phaser Scene, and CC-10.8 moves it to @couchcade/stage.
