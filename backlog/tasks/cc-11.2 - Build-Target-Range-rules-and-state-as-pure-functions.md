## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
Target Range's rules ship in games/target-range (scaffolded with pnpm create-game) as pure functions over JSON state, following docs/games/target-range.md: 1-8 players, 4 rounds x 3 volleys (near, middle, far, far and gusty) with seeded target spots and per-arrow winds, 10 s volleys that close early once everyone shot, the 500 ms late wait, closed-form arrow flight from the aim at release, rings 10..1 scored in integer tenths, points added at the reveal, placements by points then 10s, onPlayerLeft, per-phase views and a snapshot/restore keyed by player id. Tests cover flight and ring scoring, round flow, inputs, win conditions, views, snapshot, testGameContract, fast-check determinism and one recorded 3-player replay. The game-sdk contract gains an optional hidden flag that createRegistry validates but leaves out, so Target Range stays off the menu until CC-11.6 removes it.
