## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
Target Range now has its real phone controller. Players hold the phone like a remote, touch the big action and pull down to draw (full power at 150 px, fill grows, 'Full draw!'), and let go to shoot with the aim at release. Below 0.3 power, or on pointercancel, the phone sends lower and says 'Pull further to shoot'. Motion aim uses one pose tracker from the motion step's calibration into createAimDetector, recentres at every draw and streams packed samples through createAimSender and one CC-3.6 input stream, only while drawing. Phones that denied motion, have no gyroscope, stop sending samples mid-game or reloaded get the drag pad with a Centre button. Every spec screen is covered (watch intro and result, shoot, drawing, too weak, shot, clock not synced), with haptic cues. To support this, ControllerProps gains an optional motion prop ({ mode: motion, adapter, calibration } or { mode: touch }), filled in by apps/controller's runtime from the motion session and documented in platform.md. Tests cover every state, schema-valid inputs, the touch fallback and text under 40 characters. The per-game chunk is 4.84 KB of its 25 KB budget.
